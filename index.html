Our Origin Story

A Realm Where Coffee Meets Camelot

Hark, noble visitor! Château Barkalot was born from a simple quest: to build a cozy gathering place where dogs and their devoted humans could sip extraordinary brews, munch paw-worthy pastries, and forge bonds as legendary as the knights of old.

"Every great realm needs a gathering place. Ours just happens to serve very excellent coffee — and the finest pack of pups in the land."

We opened our drawbridge in the heart of Camelot City, welcoming dogs of all breeds, shapes, and energy levels. Our Round Table seats 18 (one for each noble knight), and the wag-worthy atmosphere is open to all who seek warmth, community, and a truly noble cup.

The Realm's Most Asked

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your most pressing quests.

Up to 3 dogs per table in our main hall, and up to 5 in the dedicated Play Area sessions. We recommend booking in advance if your pack is particularly noble and numerous — the Round Table fills fast on Fridays.
Dogs must be on-leash in the café and dining areas, but our dedicated Play Area is fully off-leash and padded for safe zoomies. Small and large dog zones are separated for the comfort of all knights.
All dogs must be up to date on Rabies, DHPP (distemper, parvovirus), and Bordetella (kennel cough). Please bring a copy of vaccination records on your first visit — a noble knight keeps their paperwork in order.
Entry to the café is free! The Play Area is $15/hour per dog with a one-time waiver to sign. Squire Classes and special events are priced separately — see our Events section for current rates.
For the health and safety of very young squires, puppies must be at least 16 weeks old and have completed their initial vaccine series. We welcome all new recruits to the realm — they just need to be properly prepared for the quest.
Absolutely — every knight is welcome regardless of size. Our Play Area has a dedicated large-dog zone, and our café tables are generously spaced. Irish Wolfhounds, Great Danes, and Mastiffs have all held court at our Round Table.
Click any adoptable pup's card, read their story, and click "Begin the Adoption Quest." You'll be connected with our partner shelter to complete an application. Monthly Adoption Fairs are the best time to meet pups in person — coffee on us while you chat with the shelter team.
We have 12 free spaces in our rear courtyard (enter from Paladin Lane) and a shaded area with water bowls if your pup needs a moment before entering. Street parking is also available on Round Table Way after 6pm weekdays.
Verily — we love hosting birthday barkdays, corporate team "paw-retreats," and private jousting tournaments! We can accommodate up to 40 humans and 20 dogs for private hire. Contact us and a herald will respond within one business day.
